01 · Cloud Technology Consulting

Move to AWS with confidence, then run it well.

With the cloud, even a small team can stand up enterprise-class services in minutes. The hard part is doing it securely, at a predictable cost, and in a way your team can operate. We design the landing zone, plan the migration, and stay until the workloads are stable.

  • Cloud readiness assessment and business case
  • AWS landing zone: accounts, networking, IAM, guardrails
  • Application and database migration with rollback plans
  • Infrastructure as code with Terraform and CI/CD
  • FinOps: right-sizing, reserved capacity, cost dashboards

Typical deliverables

  • Target architecture and migration wave plan
  • Terraform modules and pipeline definitions
  • Security baseline and compliance mapping
  • Cost model and monthly optimization report
  • Operations runbooks and hand-over sessions
02 · Data Engineering & Analytics

Pipelines that run every night. Numbers people trust.

Data is only useful once it is reliable, joined up, and fast to query. We build lakehouse and warehouse platforms on AWS and Snowflake, wire up batch and streaming ingestion, and put data quality checks in the path so problems surface before your stakeholders do.

  • Data platform architecture: S3, Glue, Redshift, Snowflake, Databricks
  • Batch ELT with Spark, Airflow, dbt, and Step Functions
  • Real-time processing with Kafka, Kinesis, and Spark Streaming
  • Source integration: Oracle, SQL Server, SaaS APIs, flat files
  • Data quality, lineage, and observability
  • BI and reporting with Power BI, QuickSight, or Tableau

Typical deliverables

  • Source-to-target mappings and technical design
  • Production pipelines with tests and alerting
  • Dimensional models and semantic layer
  • Performance tuning of existing pipelines and queries
  • Dashboards and self-service datasets
03 · AI & Intelligent Automation

Automate the repetitive. Augment the important.

We help organizations put large language models and machine learning to work on real operations: document processing, customer and employee support, reporting, and back-office workflows. Every solution ships with evaluation, monitoring, and human review where it matters.

  • Use-case discovery and value/feasibility scoring
  • LLM applications and agents on OpenAI, Anthropic, and AWS Bedrock
  • Retrieval-augmented generation over your documents and data
  • Workflow automation and integration with existing systems
  • Predictive models: forecasting, classification, anomaly detection
  • Guardrails, evaluation harnesses, and cost controls

Typical deliverables

  • Prioritized AI opportunity map
  • Working pilot in 4–6 weeks
  • Production deployment with monitoring
  • Prompt, model, and data governance guide
  • Training for business and technical users
04 · Modern Application Development

Cloud-native applications and integrations.

From internal tools to public-facing portals and APIs, we build applications that are secure, testable, and cheap to run. We favor managed services and serverless where they fit, and we leave you with a codebase your own developers can extend.

  • REST and GraphQL APIs with authentication and rate limiting
  • Web applications in React, Next.js, or plain modern HTML
  • System integration and API management for enterprise and education clients
  • Serverless back ends on Lambda, API Gateway, and DynamoDB
  • Automated testing, CI/CD, and observability from the first sprint

Typical deliverables

  • Solution design and API specification (OpenAPI)
  • Source code, infrastructure as code, and pipelines
  • Security review and load test results
  • User and developer documentation
05 · GIS & Utility Data Services

Spatial data support for utilities and municipalities.

Utilities and public agencies depend on accurate geographic data for assets, networks, and field operations. We provide as-needed GIS professional support, ArcGIS administration, and data engineering for spatial workloads, including utility network migrations.

  • ArcGIS Enterprise and ArcGIS Online administration
  • Geometric network to Utility Network migration planning and execution
  • Spatial ETL, data cleanup, and quality assurance
  • Integration of GIS with asset management, work order, and billing systems
  • Web maps, dashboards, and field data collection apps

Typical deliverables

  • GIS assessment and migration roadmap
  • Data model and schema documentation
  • Automated spatial pipelines
  • Staff training and knowledge transfer

Common questions

Engagement details.

How do you price engagements?

Fixed price for well-defined scopes such as assessments and migrations, and time-and-materials for ongoing build or support work. Either way you receive a written estimate before we start and a transparent invoice every month.

Do you work with public-sector procurement?

Yes. We respond to RFPs and RFQs, hold an active SAM.gov registration, and can provide W-9, insurance certificates, references, and notarized affidavits as required.

Where is your team located?

Our office is in Clarkston, Michigan, and our engineers are US-based. We work on-site in southeast Michigan when it helps and remotely everywhere else.

Will we own the code and infrastructure?

Always. Everything we build lives in your cloud accounts and your source control from the first sprint. There is no proprietary platform to license.

Can you support systems after go-live?

Yes. We offer managed support retainers for platforms we build, and we can also take over support of existing data and cloud environments after a short assessment.
