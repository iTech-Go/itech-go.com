Consent

By using our website, you consent to this Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

If you contact us directly, we may receive additional information such as your name, email address, phone number, organization, the contents of your message and any attachments or links you send, and any other information you choose to provide. Contact and application forms on this site are delivered to us by a form-processing service that transmits your submission to our email. Submissions are not sold or used for advertising. Our website is hosted on a content delivery network that may log standard request data described below.

Like most websites, itech-go.com uses log files. CCPA privacy rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, California consumers have the right to request that a business disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected, request deletion of personal data, and request that a business not sell their personal data. We do not sell personal data. If you make a request, we will respond within one month. To exercise these rights, contact us.
