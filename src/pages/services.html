Fixed price for well-defined scopes such as readiness sprints, pilots, and migrations, and time-and-materials for ongoing build or support work. You receive a written estimate before we start and a transparent invoice every month.

Yes. We respond to RFPs and RFQs from school districts, municipalities, and utilities, and we can provide the W-9, insurance certificates, references, and affidavits these processes require.

Our office is in Clarkston, Michigan. We work on-site in southeast Michigan when it helps and remotely everywhere else.

Will we own the code and infrastructure?

Always. Everything we build lives in your cloud accounts and your source control from the first sprint. There is no proprietary platform to license.

Can you support systems after go-live?

Yes. We offer managed operations retainers for AI systems and data platforms we build, and we can take over support of existing environments after a short assessment.
