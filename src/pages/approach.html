Principles

What you can hold us to.

Measure before you trust

Every AI system gets an evaluation set built from your real cases. We report accuracy, cost, and latency before launch and watch for regressions after.

Humans stay accountable

Agents prepare and execute routine steps. People approve the consequential ones. We design the gates with you, not for you.

Security is architecture

Least-privilege access, data residency, redaction, audit logs, and allow-listed actions are built in, then documented for your security team.

You own everything

Code in your repositories, infrastructure in your cloud accounts, models under your provider agreements. No platform lock-in to us.

Weeks, not quarters

Fixed-scope phases with working software at each checkpoint. You decide whether to continue based on what you see, not what you were promised.

Data first

If the data foundation is not ready, we say so and fix it first. It is the single biggest predictor of whether an AI initiative succeeds.
