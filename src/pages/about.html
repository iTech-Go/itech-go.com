About us

Engineers first. Consultants second.

iTech-Go was founded in Michigan with one mission: help organizations get real value from data, cloud, and now AI, without the overhead of a large consultancy.

Our story

Built around data. Grown into cloud. Focused on AI.

We started iTech-Go to bring a hands-on, engineering-led approach to big data and advanced analytics. Our first clients needed pipelines that ran reliably, warehouses that answered questions quickly, and people who could explain the trade-offs in plain language.

As the market moved, so did we. We helped clients adopt cloud platforms, streaming data, and advanced analytics. Today the center of our work is agentic AI: systems that read, reason, and act across a business, built on the data and cloud foundations we have spent years engineering. We serve enterprises, school districts, utilities, and government agencies.

We stay deliberately small. That keeps senior people on every engagement and keeps us accountable to the outcomes we promise.
