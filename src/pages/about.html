About us

Engineers first. Consultants second.

iTech-Go was founded in Michigan with one mission: help organizations get real value from data and cloud technology, without the overhead of a large consultancy.

Our story

Built around data. Grown into cloud, AI, and the public sector.

We started iTech-Go to bring a hands-on, engineering-led approach to big data and advanced analytics. Our first clients needed pipelines that ran reliably, warehouses that answered questions quickly, and people who could explain the trade-offs in plain language.

As the market moved, so did we. Today we deliver across the full data ecosystem and help clients adopt cloud platforms, IoT and streaming data, advanced analytics, and AI and machine learning applications. Our team of experienced engineers across North America builds and runs industry-grade data and cloud solutions for enterprises, school districts, utilities, and government agencies.

We stay deliberately small. That keeps senior people on every engagement and keeps us accountable to the outcomes we promise.
