Industries

Same engineering discipline. Your domain.

Agents, data platforms, and cloud foundations look different in a school district than in a manufacturer. Here is where we focus and what we typically build in each.

Enterprise & mid-market

Organizations with real systems of record, real compliance obligations, and a backlog of manual processes that AI can finally address.

  • Finance and operations agents: invoice processing, reconciliation, vendor onboarding
  • Employee support agents for HR and IT grounded in policy documents
  • Data platform modernization to Snowflake or Databricks with a semantic layer
  • Legacy application modernization with agentic delivery

State & local government

Michigan agencies, counties, and municipalities that need to serve residents better with the staff they have, under strict transparency and records requirements.

  • Records request and permitting workflow agents with full audit trails
  • Case management assistants that summarize and route
  • System integration and API programs across departments
  • Cloud migration and cost control for aging on-premises systems

Education

Intermediate school districts, K-12 systems, and universities modernizing the integrations and reporting that connect student, HR, and finance systems.

  • API and integration platforms between SIS, HR, finance, and state reporting
  • Staff-facing assistants for policy, benefits, and procedures
  • Governed reporting and analytics for district leadership
  • Secure cloud foundations sized for public budgets

Utilities

Water, power, and gas utilities managing GIS, asset data, work orders, and operational analytics across field and office.

  • ArcGIS support and Utility Network migrations
  • GIS-aware assistants that answer asset questions in plain language
  • Spatial and operational data pipelines into a governed platform
  • Integration of GIS with asset management, work order, and billing systems

Healthcare & life sciences

Providers and health-adjacent organizations with heavy documentation burdens and strict privacy obligations.

  • Document intelligence for referrals, authorizations, and claims with PHI safeguards
  • Private model hosting and redaction pipelines
  • Data platforms for quality reporting and operations
  • Governance packs that satisfy security and compliance reviews

Manufacturing & logistics

Operations-heavy businesses in Michigan and beyond, where quality, supply, and maintenance data are scattered across plants and systems.

  • Maintenance and quality assistants grounded in manuals and history
  • Supplier document and order processing agents
  • Streaming data platforms for plant and fleet telemetry
  • Forecasting and anomaly detection on operational data
Working with the public sector

Procurement-ready and easy to contract.

  • W-9, certificate of insurance, and references available on request
  • Transparent, itemized pricing schedules
  • Documentation written for auditors and IT security reviewers
